# R49: Resolution on the Use of X.com
> Codified after Passage at DSA Convention 08-11-2025
> [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MQyHv17FJyCqk9FMXB_mfSVQB3O6gkFhnJxmiYwdaGc/edit?pli=1&tab=t.w3ibfjqb4wyr)

## Summary
- Resolves that DSA national committees, chapters and members will deprioritize the use of X.com in favor of other communications channels.
- Resolves that DSA will explore the feasibility of collaborating with Tesla Takedown organizers on a boycott of X.com.

## Recitations
- X.com, formerly known as Twitter, is privately owned by Elon Musk who is an open fascist, a sexual predator, a racist, and the world’s richest man.
- X.com is increasingly a tool for growing the fascist movement in the United States and extending Musk’s personal control over the government.
- Reliance on X.com for public debate and private direct messaging are both major vulnerabilities for the left that could be exploited by Musk.
- The Tesla Takedown movement has emerged as an organic and effective form of opposition to Musk by targeting one of his companies with boycotts.

## Resolution
- DSA and its national committees will deprioritize the use of X.com in favor of other communications channels.
- DSA encourages DSA members and DSA chapters to deprioritize the use of X.com in favor of other communications channels.
- DSA will explore the feasibility of collaborating with Tesla Takedown organizers on a boycott of X.com.
